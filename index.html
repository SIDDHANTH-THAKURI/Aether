The gesture chain

Three moves, one flow.

Each move is read from the geometry of your hand, frame by frame — the gap between your thumb and middle finger, how far your fingertips sit from your palm, how fast that palm is travelling. No training, no recording, no lag.

01 / Ignite

Snap

Pinch thumb to middle finger and flick them apart. The sudden gap opening is the trigger — a spark cracks to life on your fingertips.

02 / Hold

Cup

Open your palm and the spark drops into it, growing into a fireball that charges the longer you hold. Close your hand and it snuffs out.

03 / Release

Throw

Flick your hand and it launches along the exact path you threw it, arcing down before it detonates. Charge decides the size of the blast.
